**First author**: **Junteng Liu**, Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He

This work discovers and verifies the existence of a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models that governs their factuality judgment, published at EMNLP 2024. This work has an associated open source GitHub repository: Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane.
